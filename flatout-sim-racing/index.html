Flatout Sim Racing × LVRY

Located inside LVRY at Wildfire Raceway, Flatout delivers a high-end virtual racing experience that puts you behind the wheel in a whole new way. Advanced full-motion simulators let you take on iconic cars and tracks with realism that mirrors real-world performance.

Whether you’re learning the basics or chasing faster lap times, Flatout offers an interactive environment where anyone can step into the role of a driver — a natural extension of the campus experience.

  • 160+ race cars — Formula, NASCAR, IndyCar, GT, sports cars & more
  • World-class tracks — COTA, Daytona, Le Mans, Spa, Monza & hundreds more via iRacing
  • Full-motion rigs — feel every corner, curb, and straightaway blast
